Publications

Publications by Pangmiaomiao Zhang in reversed chronological order.

2026

  1. Nat. Commun.
    Real-Time Visualization of G2L4 Reverse Transcriptase in DNA Repair via Microhomology-Mediated End Joining
    Pangmiaomiao Zhang, Mo Guo, Y. Jessie Zhang, and 2 more authors
    Nature Communications, 2026
    Accepted manuscript; preprint posted on bioRxiv in March 2026
    Abs Bib Website

    Using high-speed atomic force microscopy and biochemical assays, we visualized G2L4 reverse transcriptase-mediated DNA repair through microhomology-mediated end joining, including DNA intermediates and ligase activity.

2024

  1. Biophys. J.
    Real-Time Visualization of Intermolecular Interactions between SARS-CoV-2 Spike Protein and NTD-Binding Antibody Using High-Speed Atomic Force Microscopy
    Pangmiaomiao Zhang, Chia-Wei Chou, Ilya J. Finkelstein, and 1 more author
    Biophysical Journal, 2024
    Poster presentation at the Biophysical Society Annual Meeting 2024
    Abs Bib

    Visualized single spike protein, ACE2 and neutralizing antibody (NTD binder) using high-speed atomic force microscopy (HS-AFM) and compared with simulated AFM images. Analyzed single molecule dynamics of neutralizing antibody and investigated interactions between spike protein and neutralizing antibody.

2022

  1. Anal. Chem.
    Cross-Reactive Fluorescent Sensor Array for Discrimination of Amyloid Beta Aggregates
    Pangmiaomiao Zhang and Chunyan Tan
    Analytical Chemistry, 2022
    Abs Bib

    Prepared different aggregation states of amyloid beta peptide and characterized them using transmission electron microscopy (TEM) and CD spectrometer. Discriminated different aggregation states using fluorescence sensor array containing conjugated polyelectrolytes and small molecules, analyzed by PCA and LDA algorithms.

2020

  1. ACS ABM
    Fluorescence Sensor Array for Discrimination of Urine Proteins and Differentiation Diagnosis of Urinary System Diseases
    Yishun Shen, Yuanfang Huang, Pangmiaomiao Zhang, and 4 more authors
    ACS Applied Bio Materials, 2020
    Abs Bib

    Constructed three-unit fluorescence sensor array and realized discrimination of six common urine proteins, their different concentrations and their complex mixtures. Distinguished protein profiles of urine samples from healthy people and patients with different urinary system diseases.

2019

  1. Asian J. Org. Chem.
    Phosphine Catalyzed Enantioselective [1+4]-Annulation of Morita-Baylis-Hillman Carbonates with α,β-Unsaturated Imines
    Chenxiao Qian, Pangmiaomiao Zhang, Wenjun Li, and 1 more author
    Asian Journal of Organic Chemistry, 2019
    Abs Bib

    Established an organic methodology using phosphine catalyst for [4+1] cycloaddition of unsaturated imine and MBH ester with a wide range of substituent groups to obtain chiral 2-pyrrolines with high yields and good enantioselectivities.

2017

  1. ChemistrySelect
    A Catalyst-Free Cycloaddition Reaction: Access to Spiro[chroman-3,2’-indene-1’,3’-dione] Scaffolds
    Yuzhe Han, Yingzu Zhu, Pangmiaomiao Zhang, and 2 more authors
    ChemistrySelect, 2017
    Abs Bib

    Constructed a facile [3+2] cycloaddition reaction to obtain spiro[chroman-3,2’-indene-1’,3’-dione] incorporating two biologically active scaffolds without need of catalysts and complex operation. Products with high yields were obtained and characterized by NMR, HPLC and X-ray crystallography.
