**Status:** First-author manuscript accepted in *Nature Communications*; [bioRxiv preprint](https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.03.13.711756)

This project combines biochemical assays and high-speed atomic force microscopy (HS-AFM) to investigate how G2L4 reverse transcriptase participates in DNA repair through microhomology-mediated end joining (MMEJ).

#### Key Contributions

- Designed DNA oligonucleotide substrates with microhomology regions for DNA repair assays
- Optimized biochemical and single-molecule workflows to characterize **reverse transcriptase-DNA interactions**, repair intermediates, and ligase activity
- Visualized G2L4-mediated MMEJ and T4 DNA ligase activity using HS-AFM
